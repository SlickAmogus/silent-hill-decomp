# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the **Silent Hill AI-Assisted PC Port**.

## Ownership & Governance

This project (specifically, this AI Assisted Silent Hill port, not the decompilation or Silent Hill itself) is owned solely by **Chris Hardin (AKA KushAstronaut)**. Chris is the
sole owner and maintainer, and **final decisions about the project's direction,
scope, features, and releases rest with him.**  He does not have any rights to Silent Hill as a property, and this project does not contain any game assets.

## Official Communication

Official information, announcements, builds, and decisions regarding this project
come **only** from one of the following sources:

- **Chris Hardin (KushAstronaut)** directly
- The official project website: <https://sh1pc.com/>
- This **GitHub repository**
- Chris's **YouTube channel** (KushAstronaut)
- Chris's posts in the **project's thread in the PS1 Decompilation Discord**

If you see information about this project anywhere else, or attributed to anyone
else as "official," please treat it as unofficial and unverified until confirmed
through one of the channels above.

## How You Can Contribute

Contributions are genuinely welcome. You are encouraged to:

- **Submit pull requests**
- **Report bugs** (please include steps to reproduce, your OS, and logs where possible)
- **Share feedback, ideas, and suggestions**

Contributors will be **credited accordingly** for their work.

That said, this is not a consensus-run project. While contributions and feedback
are valued, **final project decisions are made by Chris Hardin** — whether a given
contribution is accepted, deferred, reworked, or declined is ultimately his call.

## Forking

Anyone is welcome to **fork** this project if they have their own ideas for how it
should be operated, maintained, or where it should go. That is the nature of open
development — if your vision differs, you are free to build it.

## Credits & Setup

This port is built on top of the PSX decompilation and the
[PsyCross](https://github.com/OpenDriver2/PsyCross) compatibility layer. See the
[README](README.md) for build instructions, current status, and additional credits.
